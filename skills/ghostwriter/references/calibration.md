# Calibration Guide

## Sample handling

Ask for user-authored examples in different situations. Do not pressure the user to provide sensitive or confidential material. Encourage redaction of names, identifiers, and proprietary details.

## Eight dimensions

1. Tone and formality.
2. Sentence architecture and rhythm.
3. Vocabulary, jargon, and language mixing.
4. Punctuation and spacing.
5. Formatting and layout.
6. Opening approach.
7. Framing of problems and negative news.
8. Explicit dislikes and constraints.

## Confidence

For each observation, distinguish:
- **Observed:** directly supported by multiple examples.
- **Tentative:** suggested by one example.
- **Unknown:** insufficient evidence.

Do not present tentative observations as permanent traits. Ask the user to confirm the profile. Store only the confirmed profile unless the user separately opts into sample retention.
